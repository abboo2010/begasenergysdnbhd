BEGAS Energy Sdn Bhd was incorporated in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, on 4 September 2008. Its foundation is in engineering and construction, with civil, mechanical and electrical services as the core disciplines.

The company concentrates on maintenance services for oil and gas majors operating in the upstream and downstream sectors, supporting both onshore facilities and offshore operations.

Services range from HVAC operation and maintenance, valves maintenance and switchboard fabrication to PLC-based systems, piping and structural repair, civil maintenance, VSAT data transmission, pipeline rehabilitation and manpower supply.

BEGAS positions itself as a trusted engineering and construction partner, committed to reliability, innovation and the standards that oil and gas clients expect.
